How we rate fit

Every card is checked against the same rules, for a NYC renovation with kids. A rating counts only if it appears on the vendor's own product page; vendor tags and marketing copy have contradicted the page before. Each rating is computed by a script from typed facts, and “checks passed” on every card shows which ones it used.

By use

  • Deck: freeze/thaw stated as passing; DCOF 0.42 or higher wet (or R10+); matte, satin or honed, never polished or gloss.
  • Bath or kitchen floor: DCOF 0.42 or higher stated (matte 0.50+ preferred); not polished or gloss; non-porous, or a one-time seal.
  • Walls and backsplash: glazed or sealed and wipeable. No frost or slip rating needed.
  • Wallpaper: at least spongeable, scrubbable for kid height; not in bathrooms; below grade only after any damp is fixed.

For every item

  • Good fit: passes every gate for its use. In stock or ships in under 4 weeks; no minimum, or under 25 sq ft; a sample is sold.
  • Use with care: misses one gate, or has a missing or conflicting rating a vendor email could settle. Ships in 4–12 weeks, carries a small-batch fee, or needs regular resealing.
  • Poor fit (hidden by default): fails a hard gate: freeze/thaw fails on a deck, DCOF under 0.42 on a floor, over 12 weeks to ship, or a 100 sq ft or larger minimum.

The shipping and minimum-order limits are working assumptions and can change. The full rules are in REQUIREMENTS.md and the numbers in fit-rules.json.

Prices, stock and ship times come from each vendor's product page and change often. Confirm before ordering. Suitability notes (frost, slip) are the vendor's own listings, not an installer's review.
